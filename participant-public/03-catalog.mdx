# Training Catalog

:::warning[Under construction]

This content is still in development. Please check in the future for updates.

:::

The PCGL Training Team and Patient Partnership Team have created a catalog of resources for research participants and members of the public.
The catalog includes resources developed by PCGL and external resources offered by other organizations. Most resources are for adults, but resources for children are noted in the description.

Browse the Training Catalog for research participants and members of the public below.
